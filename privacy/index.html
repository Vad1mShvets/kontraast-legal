Privacy Policy: Rock Band Simulator

Last updated: 26 October 2026

The short version. Rock Band Simulator works offline. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything it saves stays on your computer. The only things that leave it are things you choose: your achievements go to Steam, your saves sync through Steam Cloud if you have that turned on, and the game can show a "Playing Rock Band Simulator" status in your Discord app.

1. Who we are

KONTRAAST MEDIA LIMITED, trading as KONTRĀST MEDIA ("we", "us"), a company registered in Ireland. We make Rock Band Simulator and its demo. We are the data controller for everything in this policy.

Contact for anything privacy-related: kontraastmedia@gmail.com

The game is published on Steam together with PlayWay S.A. The game does not send any data to the publisher.

2. What the game keeps on your computer

These files never leave your computer unless Steam Cloud is on (see 3.1). We cannot see them.

WhatWhat is in it
Saves (up to three profiles)Your progress, money, items and choices in the story, the band name you typed, and a small screenshot of the moment you saved. The game also keeps a few automatic backups.
SettingsVolume, mouse and gamepad sensitivity, language, graphics options.
Game logUnity, the engine the game is built on, writes a technical log file (Player.log) with the game's messages and file paths on your computer. It helps if the game crashes. We only see it if you choose to send it to us.

Windows: %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\KONTRAAST MEDIA\Rock Band Simulator macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/KONTRAAST MEDIA/Rock Band Simulator

To delete your data, delete your profiles in the game's menu, or delete that folder. Uninstalling the game through Steam may leave the folder behind.

3. What leaves your computer

3.1 Steam

The game talks to the Steam client you are running. It sends Steam the achievements you unlock. If you have Steam Cloud switched on for the game, Steam copies your save files (profiles, backups and their thumbnails, but not your settings) to your Steam account so you can continue on another computer. The game does not read your Steam name, friends list or purchases. How Steam handles this data is described in Valve's Privacy Policy.

3.2 Discord status (Rich Presence)

If the Discord desktop app is running on your computer, the game gives it a status to show. It contains:

  • what you are doing and where: "In the main menu", the place in the game ("Drinking at the bar"), the story day, "In a brawl", "At the computer";
  • during a concert, "On stage with" and the band name you typed;
  • how long you have been playing;
  • two buttons: the game's Steam page and our Discord server invite.

This goes from the game to your own Discord app, on your computer, over a local connection. Your Discord app then shows it to other people according to your Discord activity settings. We do not receive it. The game does not use or keep your Discord name, ID, friends or messages. To stop it, turn off "Share your detected activity with others" in Discord (User Settings, Activity Privacy), or close Discord while you play. Note that your band name is text you typed yourself and can be seen by others on your Discord profile while you are on stage.

3.3 Links

The game has buttons that open our Steam page and Discord invite in your browser or Steam. Once they open, that website's own policy applies.

3.4 What the game does not do

No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reports, no advertising, no accounts, no online play. Unity's own analytics, crash reporting and performance reporting services are switched off in the game.

4. Our Discord server and bug reports

Our community Discord server is run on Discord's platform. What you post there is visible to the server's members and is handled by Discord under Discord's Privacy Policy. We read posts and attachments (screenshots, logs) to find and fix bugs, answer questions and keep the server safe.

To do that we use a private Discord app (a bot) that only we can use and that exists only in our server. It can read the channels it has been given and nothing else. It never posts, replies, reacts, sends direct messages or joins other servers. We keep copies of bug reports (the text, your Discord username and any attachments) on our own computers while we work on them. We use software tools to help us sort and summarise reports. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising or use it to profile anyone.

We use this data because we have a legitimate interest in fixing the game and supporting players. We keep it for as long as it is useful for fixing a problem or answering you, then delete it. You can ask us to delete your messages from our copies at any time (section 8). Messages on Discord itself can be deleted by you or by the server's moderators.

5. Email

If you email us, we use your address and your message to reply and to follow up on what you wrote. We keep the conversation for as long as it is useful, and delete it when it no longer is.

6. Why we may use your data (GDPR)

  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)): bug reports, support, and moderation in our Discord server and email.
  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)): the optional Discord status is something you turn on or off yourself in Discord; saves and settings on your computer are part of the game you chose to play.

7. Who else handles data

We do not sell personal data. Data may be handled by: Discord (the server), Valve (Steam, achievements, Steam Cloud), our email provider, and the software tools mentioned in section 4. Some of these are in the United States, and rely on their own safeguards for transfers out of the EU, such as standard contractual clauses.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask us to: show you the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, limit how we use it, give you a copy, or stop using it. You may withdraw consent at any time. Email kontraastmedia@gmail.com and we will answer within one month. The game itself stores nothing with us, so for the data in section 2 the quickest way to delete it is to delete the folder. For data held by Steam or Discord, contact them.

You can also complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie), or to the data protection authority of your own country.

9. Children

The game contains mature themes (drugs, alcohol, violence, crude humour) and is meant for players aged 16 and over. Discord requires its users to be at least 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you think a child has posted in our server or written to us, tell us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Everything the game saves is on your computer, so its safety depends on your computer. The copies we keep of bug reports are on our own devices, and the accounts that can reach the Discord server's data are protected with two-factor authentication.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change matters to you (for example, if the game starts sending something anywhere), we will say so in the game's update notes.
